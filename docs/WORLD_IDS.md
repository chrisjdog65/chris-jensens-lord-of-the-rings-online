## Conventions (details in the header of 05_data_world.js)
- Ids: towns/zones plain; NPCs `npc_<town>_<short>`; spawns `sp_<zone>_<n>`; gather nodes `gn_<zone>_<n>`; fishing spots `fs_*`; POIs `poi_*`; bosses `boss_<type>` (boss monster type id = `<type>`); docks `dock_*`; roads `road_<from>_<to>`.
- Yaw radians, 0 faces −Z; building doors face forward. Interior NPCs: `interior:{town,index}` + listed in the building's `npcInside`. Boat-masters carry `dock` and stand at the dock. Hub quest-givers carry `hub:true`.
- Water model `G.Data.world.water` / `isWater(x,z)`: sea x<−1500 or z<−1650 except the islands (Tol Fuin r187, Himling r204, Tol Morwen r153), the Forochel landmass (r300 at 600,−1850; ice-bay r130 at 450,−1980) and the isle of Tinnudir (r90 at −200,−1050 in lake Nenuial, reached by causeway roads). Rivers 12 m wide (Brandywine, Hoarwell); road crossings are fords/bridges.
- Walled towns (`walls:true`): bree (wallRadius 122, gates W/S/E), archet, aughaire, chetwoodcamp (palisades) — 13_buildings builds the ring; the data has no wall pieces.
- Every monster type/boss references `G.Data.monsterAbilities` ids from 04_data_abilities. Travel routes: every ordered pair of the 16 stable towns, cost = 150 + 1.1 × distance (copper).
- Race start towns: hobbit/riverhobbit → hobbiton (−1040,−120); man/dúnadan/rohirrim/beorning → archet (−60,−250); elf/high elf → celondim (−1410,−690); dwarf/stout-axe → thorinshall (−1290,−1090).
